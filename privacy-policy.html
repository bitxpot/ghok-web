Ghok privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Ghok is a farm record app for poultry, goat, pig and cattle farms. It is made by [YOUR FULL NAME], Accra, Ghana ("we"). This page explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your phone number To sign you in with a one-time SMS code, and so a farm owner can add you to their team.
Your name and your role on a farm (owner, manager, worker, advisor) So the farm knows who did each task and who can see what.
Farm records you or your team enter: farm name and town, pens, animals and tags, counts, weights, feeding, health and medicine records, tasks, sales, expenses, buyer names and phone numbers, and messages between team members This is the purpose of the app: keeping the farm's records and sharing them with the farm's team.
Photos taken in the app with the camera Proof that certain jobs were done (for example giving a vaccine or cleaning a house). The app only uses the camera when you tap to take a photo; it does not read your gallery.
Subscription status (plan, paid until) To know whether the farm's plan is active.
Taps on Call or WhatsApp in the Services list To count how many farmers each listed vet or supplier receives.

We do not collect your location, contacts, gallery, advertising ID, or browsing activity, and the app shows no advertising.

Payments

When you pay through Google Play, Google handles the payment; we receive only confirmation that the subscription is paid and until when. If you pay us directly (for example by Paystack, mobile money or bank), the payment provider handles your payment details; we receive the amount, a reference and your farm code. We never see your card or mobile-money PIN.

Who can see your farm's data

  • People on the farm's team, according to their role. Workers see their pens and tasks; only the owner, and managers the owner allows, see money.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, only to provide the service: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage), Africa's Talking (sending sign-in SMS codes), Google (Google Play purchases) and Paystack (direct payments).
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On our server, access rules check every request so that each person only sees the farms they belong to. Records are also kept on your phone so the app works without signal.

How long we keep data

Farm records are kept while the farm's account exists. When an account is deleted (see below), the data is removed, except payment records, which we keep without personal details for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Menu › Delete my account. Or follow the steps on this page.

  • If you own a farm, the farm and all its records, photos and team list are deleted for everyone.
  • If you are on someone else's farm, your name and phone number are removed from it; the records you entered stay with the farm owner.
  • Your sign-in account is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Contact us using the details below. A farm owner can also export the farm's records from the app.

Children

Ghok is a tool for farm work and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell users in the app.
