Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Ghok is a farm management app for poultry, goats, pigs and cattle, made in Ghana. Ghok Eye is our camera box that counts birds and watches pens. This page explains what information we collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can have it deleted. “We” and “us” means Ghok.

What we collect

  • Your phone number and name. You sign in with your phone number and a code we send by SMS. Your name shows to the people on your farm.
  • Your farm's records. Farm name and town, pens, animals, daily records (eggs, feed, deaths, medicine, weights), tasks, money in and out, shopping lists, and messages between people on the farm.
  • Your team. Names and phone numbers of workers, managers and advisors that the owner or a manager adds.
  • Photos. Photos you take in the app (for example of a sick animal). If your farm has Ghok Eye: pictures of your pens from your cameras, and, when Ghok Guard is on, a picture when a camera sees a person at night. These pictures can show people who are in the pen.
  • Payment records. Which plan was paid for, the amount, the date, and the payment reference. We never see or store your card or mobile money PIN.
  • Basic technical information. The app version and when your phone last synced, so we can fix problems.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or anything from other apps. We do not show ads.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: keep your farm's records, share them with the people on your farm, and work out tasks, alerts and insights.
  • To count birds and send Ghok Guard alerts, if your farm has Ghok Eye.
  • To sign you in, give each phone number one free month, and keep track of your plan and payments.
  • To help you when you contact us, and to fix faults.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your farm's information

  • People on your farm, according to their role. The owner decides who joins. Workers see their own pens and tasks; money is shown only to the owner and to managers the owner allows.
  • Ghok staff, only when needed to give support, fix a fault, or set up and look after a Ghok Eye box.

How long we keep it

  • Farm records: for as long as the farm has an account, so you can look back at past batches. If your plan ends, the records stay and you can still read and export them.
  • Ghok Eye counting pictures: deleted after 30 days once used, and after 90 days if never used. Ghok Guard pictures: deleted after 90 days.
  • When an account is deleted, its information is removed (see below). We keep payment records as the law requires for accounting, and a one-way code made from the phone number so the free month is given only once.

Keeping it safe

  • Information travels between the app and our servers encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each farm can only be read by its own people; our servers check this on every request.
  • Ghok Eye boxes keep the camera passwords on the box. They are never sent to us.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export: the owner can export the farm's records from Plan & billing → Export my records.
  • Correct: edit or remove records in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app (farm menu → Delete my account), or follow the steps on our delete your account page.
  • Turn off Ghok Eye or Ghok Guard at any time in the app.

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask what information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to the Data Protection Commission if you are not happy with how we handled it.

Children

Ghok is for farmers and farm workers aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters for how your information is used, we will tell you in the app.
