Delete your Ghok account

Ghok is a farm management app for poultry, goats, pigs and cattle.

Fastest: in the app

  1. Open Ghok and tap your farm name at the top of Today.
  2. Tap Delete my account at the bottom of the menu.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

Send a message from the phone number you use to sign in to Ghok saying “Delete my Ghok account” to:

  • WhatsApp: +233 00 000 0000
  • Email: support@example.com (include your phone number)

We confirm the number belongs to you, then delete the account within 7 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • If you own a farm: the farm and everything recorded on it — pens, animals, daily records, weights, money, messages and team list — for everyone on that farm.
  • If you work on someone else's farm: your name and phone number are removed from that farm. The records you entered stay with the farm, because they belong to the farm owner.
  • Your sign-in and phone number.

What is kept

  • Records of payments (amount, date, plan), for accounting and tax, as the law requires.
  • A one-way code made from your phone number, only so the free month is given once per number.

If you pay through Google Play, cancel the subscription in Google Play as well; deleting your account does not stop Google from charging.
